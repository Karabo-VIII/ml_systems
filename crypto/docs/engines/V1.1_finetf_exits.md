## The diagnosis (correct + still standing)
The fine-TF failure is **round-trip COST FROM CHURN**: cost-as-%-of-gross runs 0.8% → 4.6% → 14.8% → 30% → 70% →
206% across 1d→15m, while GROSS stays +100%+ at every TF and capture-rate of 5%+ moves is FLAT ~0.36–0.41. So the
killer is over-trading, NOT under-participation or late entry. The constructive lever is churn reduction — which is
exactly why the (leaky) `htf_entry_gate` looked so good and why, de-leaked, only the drawdown property survives.

## The leak (the make-or-break, FAIL → fixed)
`htf_entry_gate` (the universal lever — 16/16 of the 2020 winners used an HTF rule) had **look-ahead**:
- Chimera bar timestamps are jittered CLOSE times; the loader floors them to the **window-START** label.
- `align_htf_on` searchsorted on those START labels and returned the coarser bar **containing** base-time *t* — whose
  window closes in the FUTURE. So a 1h bar at 12:00 was gated by the 4h bar spanning [12:00,16:00) that does not
  close until 16:00. `W.shift(1)` removed only one fine bar of a ~(coarse/fine − 1)-bar leak.
- **Proof:** strict-lagging the coarse band by one full coarse bar collapses HMA|1h **+879% → +109%** (slice7d
  0.868 → 0.509) and TEMA|30m **+2641% → +88%**. (`_audit_htf_leak.json`)

**Fix (committed):** `align_htf_on` now aligns on coarser **CLOSE-times** (start-label + nominal bar width), so a
coarser bar is usable only after it has closed. Selftest updated to the causal expectation; de-leaked HMA|1h = +116%
/ slice7d 0.53, matching the auditor's independent recomputation to the decimal.

## Verdict
**Fine-TF exit-harness + HTF-gate is REFUTED as a fine-TF wealth edge.** "Profitable in every 7d slice across all

## Lessons (binding)
1. **HTF / multi-TF alignment is a look-ahead trap.** Any coarser-TF signal aligned onto a finer grid MUST align on
   the coarser bar's CLOSE-time, never its start-label. A single-bar lag is NOT enough when coarse/fine ≫ 1.
2. **A bull-year absolute rate is a low bar.** Always-long cleared slice7d 0.62 in 2021 — judge by LIFT over an
   always-long / band_off control, never the absolute ≥0.50.
3. **"Adds value at finer cadence" must beat the coarse engine, not just a flat baseline.** The fine TF was redundant.
4. The operating model's adversarial-verify caught its 3rd fabrication; the per-worker self-diagnosis ("all HOLD!")
   was wrong; the dispatched red-team was right. Keep the build→adversarial-verify→GATE loop.
